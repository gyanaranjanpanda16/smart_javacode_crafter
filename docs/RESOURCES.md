# Resources and scope

Use these as supporting material; the repository plan is an authored curriculum, not an official company syllabus or a claim about current question frequency.

- [Google Careers](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/): role requirements and application information. Confirm interview format and level expectations with your recruiter.
- [Amazon interview topics](https://amazon.jobs/content/en-gb/how-we-hire/interview-prep/software-development-topics): an official example of broad software-interview preparation areas.
- [Java 21 API](https://docs.oracle.com/en/java/javase/21/docs/api/): collection contracts, concurrency utilities and language APIs.
- [Google SRE book](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/): reliability and distributed-system operations.
- [LeetCode problem set](https://leetcode.com/problemset/): search the named exercises; platform access may vary. Timed and original assignments need no paid platform.
- [JUnit guide](https://docs.junit.org/current/user-guide/): testing reference.

Do not memorize designs. Derive them from requirements and verify uncertain technical details in primary documentation.
